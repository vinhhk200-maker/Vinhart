Vinh's Speeches

01. The Reality of UX

"Don't confuse UX with art or random creativity. In reality, UX is about learning from what’s already proven. Applying successful patterns is the fastest way to avoid expensive mistakes.

UX is logic. Stop guessing. A design isn't finished on the drawing board; it needs to be fed with data. A/B testing isn't just 'polite feedback'—it’s the hard truth from real users. That’s what real improvement looks like."

02. The Soul of UI

"UI isn't just about making things look cool. It's about identity. Whether a design has 'good taste' or not depends on how you shape a style using common design languages.

Systems scale, hacks fail. Fonts, colors, icons... everything must live inside a Design System. Without a solid system, a brand has no soul."

03. AI: Tools vs. Human Value

"AI Agents are great for running a business, but Generative AI is flooding the internet with 'trash' every day. Now that anyone can create an image with a few prompts, 'hand-crafted' human work becomes a true luxury.

AI handles the grind, you lead the mind. Instead of spending thousands on developers, I use my own logic and AI Agents to build exactly what I want. This is an opportunity, not a barrier."
