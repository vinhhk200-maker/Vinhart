UX: Inheritance, Not Art
Don't confuse UX with art. It's about learning from what works to avoid paying the price of failure.
The Truth in Data
UX is logic. Stop guessing. It doesn't stop at a drawing; it must be fed by data.

UX is not creativity—it is inheritance. It is logic and objective analysis that guides users toward decisions. Good UX means rapidly learning from successful patterns instead of paying the price of failure ourselves. A design is never finished at the mockup stage; it must be fed by the brutal truth of data via A/B testing, rather than relying on luck or polite opinions. That is the essence of improvement.

UI: Identity & Style
UI isn't just decoration. It's identity. A design with 'taste' defines its style on common languages.
Systems Scale, Hacks Fail
Systems scale, hacks fail. Without a Design System, a brand has no soul.

UI isn't just about making things look interesting. It is identity. Whether a design has 'taste' or not lies in defining a style upon common design languages.

Systems scale, hacks fail. Fonts, colors, or icons... everything must exist within a Design System. Without a system, a brand has no soul. It also drives operational efficiency, drastically reducing the time and cost needed for future changes and customization.

Hand-Drafted Luxury
When anyone can generate images with a prompt, human 'hand-drafted' work becomes a luxury.
Opportunity, Not Barrier
AI handles the grind, you lead the mind. Use code logic and AI Agents to bring ideas to life yourself.

AI Agents are the most useful for professional use, but Generative AI is creating too much 'trash' on the internet because those services are designed for everyone as tools and toys. Consequently, a deep human 'hand-drafted' touch becomes a luxury—and for me, this is a huge opportunity.

AI handles the grind, you lead the mind. Today, instead of burning capital on a large development team, AI Agents handle the same workload. My focus shifts purely to creativity and effective direction, ensuring ideas are realized rapidly.

Creative Production Management The Core Principles

1. The Balancing Act

The core of creative production management is maintaining a constant balance between available resources, project budget, and client or company needs. I believe in putting everything on paper to see the whole picture—which is why professional tools like Trello, Jira, or Monday are essential.

2. The 3-Step Execution Rule

My target for every project follows a simple, three-part framework:

  • Make it work (Functionality)
  • Make it right (Quality)
  • Make it “Wow” (Crazy Creativity)

3. The 70/30 Strategy

I dedicate 70% of the focus to hitting the core targets (Work & Right). The remaining 30% is reserved strictly for creative breakthroughs.

4. Budget vs. Creativity

The main difference between a low-budget project and a big investment isn't the goal—it’s how we express creativity.

Small budgets require creative solutions to overcome limits. Big investments use creativity to build scale and deliver wow experiences.
