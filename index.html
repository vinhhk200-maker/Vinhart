Case Study (Portfolio)

IKA Beauty: Optimized Operations & Effective Marketing.

As a solo startup, I leverage technology and art-craft expertise to build premium brands with minimal costs. I bridge the gap between strategic precision and cinematic execution, delivering agency-level quality through a strictly results-driven model.

  • I streamline operations via automated workflows using Google Workspace and AI Agents, bypassing expensive management software while maximizing efficiency.
  • For production, I’ve architected a high-quality BRAW database from a single, comprehensive setup. By applying batch Color Space Transforms, I create optimized, cinematic assets that designers use independently. This removes me as a bottleneck and provides a full year of marketing content without repetitive shooting.
  • My strategy prioritizes curiosity and empathy over hard-selling. By using vibe-driven visuals to filter potential customers, I ensure they proactively seek the brand.

The Final Impact: I successfully systemized and handed over the entire operation. The brand is now self-sustaining with just one independent designer, driven by high customer loyalty and consistent organic referrals.
